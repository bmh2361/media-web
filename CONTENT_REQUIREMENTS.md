# Content Requirements Before Launch

The following items are intentionally not represented as public claims until they are supplied, approved, or reviewed:

- Legal entity name, registered address, governing law, privacy controller, processors, retention periods, and legal review of Privacy and Terms.
- Approved leadership names, biographies, and contact channels.
- Approved client case studies, client names, project facts, usage permissions, and disclosure status.
- Approved social profile URLs and real project media with confirmed rights and captions.
- Production contact webhook destination and the exact browser origins permitted to submit enquiries.

Keep concept examples labelled as illustrative until a client-approved record replaces them.
