# Chinese–English translation issues

Chinese is the authoritative source text. This log separates source-fidelity problems from English copy-quality improvements.

## Priority fixes

Correct these source-meaning changes first:

1. `机械执行` rendered as “translating.”
2. `中欧沟通` and `中英双语沟通` rendered as China-to-Europe concepts.
3. `拓展` rendered as “activate.”
4. `商务与执行责任` reduced to commercial responsibility.
5. `判断` rendered as market learning, decisions, or framing.
6. `项目路径` rendered as a delivery brief.
7. `发布` rendered as press.
8. `英国/欧洲场景` rendered as UK/European-facing.
9. Privacy and terms wording that alters legal or data-handling meaning.

## Repeated terminology to standardise

| Chinese term | Problematic English renderings | Preferred treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `判断` | judgement, decision, market learning, framing | Use **judgement**, **assessment**, or **insight** as context requires; do not substitute decision, learning, or framing without source support. |
| `市场信息` | market learning | Use **market information**, **market intelligence**, or **market insights.** |
| `拓展` | grow; activate | Use **grow**, **develop**, or **expand**; reserve activation for `激活`. |

| `行业参与` | industry presence | Use **industry engagement** or **industry participation.** |

## English copy quality

These entries are understandable translations, but should be edited for idiomatic British commercial English. They are not, by themselves, material source-fidelity errors.

| Current English | More natural English |
| --- | --- |
| **showing the business moment** | **showing the commercial context** / **key commercial milestone** |
| **Turn a fixed date into a joined-up programme** | **Build a coordinated programme around a fixed date** |
| **Use industry, expert, institutional or content environments** | **Engage the right industry, expert, institutional and content channels** |
| **delivery layer** | **local delivery capability** / **local execution support** |
| **after the live moment** | **after the event** |
| **One joined-up local route** | **One coordinated local delivery path** |
| **Local action assembled around the commercial result** | **Local execution built around the commercial objective** |
| **When the UK or European opportunity needs to move** | **When it’s time to move a UK or European opportunity forward** |
| **one working route** | **one coordinated workflow** |
| **Engage the required … relationships** | **Engage the relevant buyers, partners, industry contacts and specialists** |
| **a cross-border commercial partnerships and market-execution team** | **a cross-border team focused on commercial partnerships and market execution** |
| **Corporate relationships & requirement discovery** | **Corporate relationships & requirements gathering** |
| **Local relationships advanced** | **Local relationships actively managed** |
| **Live delivery owned** | **Clear ownership of on-site delivery** |
| **Read the technical presentation environment** | **Assess the technical presentation setting** |
| **UK-located automotive brand imagery** | **on-location automotive brand imagery in the UK** |
| **Read the live stage and audience context** | **Assess the live stage and audience setting** |
| **A focused two-image public record establishing the performers…** | **A focused two-image public record clearly showing the performers and the London live setting.** |
| **Editorial fashion evidence** | **Editorial fashion imagery** |
| **A selected capability set** | **A curated portfolio showcasing…** |
| **a useful view of capability** | **a clear demonstration of our capabilities** |
| **non-duplicative product and portrait evidence** | **distinct product and portrait imagery** |
| **vehicle-in-motion and environmental image capability** | **our ability to capture vehicles in motion across varied European environments** |

The case-study copy also overuses **evidence**, **capability**, **market moment**, **live delivery**, **local route**, **context**, **on the ground**, **assets**, and **joined-up**. Reduce these abstractions in favour of direct, concrete English.
