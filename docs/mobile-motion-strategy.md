# Mobile Motion Strategy

Test targets: 320, 360, 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1024 pixels.

- Sticky capability storytelling becomes a natural vertical sequence.
- Custom pointer and magnetic effects are disabled.
- Parallax resolves to zero.
- Hero media remains layered but uses a bounded minimum height and readable text measure.
- Moving industry context is slower; reduced motion becomes a wrapping list.
- Hover project previews become direct media cards and links.
- Contact modes and full-brief steps use large controls and native inputs.
- Tables remain horizontally scrollable with a labelled region.
- Safe-area and viewport units are used for full-screen navigation.
- No section requires scroll hijacking or forced horizontal gesture navigation.
