# Phase 3.2D page redesign decisions

## Frozen architecture

The positioning, primary navigation, four capabilities, four commercial project types, CTA destinations, evidence governance and claim gates are unchanged. Decisions below affect presentation, media selection and concise supporting copy only.

## Home

- Rebalanced the hero to a 7/5 editorial grid and widened the English and Chinese headline measures.
- Replaced the desktop lead frame with the stronger approved Changan cover image; technology remains the mobile static lead.
- Changed the early project rail from four equal small cards to one dominant project plus visible continuation.
- Retained the four outcomes but paired each with an explicit proof scene and a larger active visual field.
- Replaced the 2×2 capability-card collage with four editorial index rows connected to one active field.
- Established a 8/4/7 selected-work hierarchy.
- Kept the three-step process and differentiators but increased readable scale.
- Replaced the large ochre CTA with a near-black conclusion.

## Capabilities

- Opened with a large proposition, four project-world rows and one contextual field.
- Kept the institutional chapter primarily typographic and increased its evidence density.
- Reduced the industry sequence from five to four authoritative moments and increased image scale.
- Reduced the creator collage from six to three moments: one dominant campaign frame and two supports.
- Reduced production from six collage items to four output-led frames on a 7/5 rhythm.
- Preserved the four commercial project routes while retaining their expandable buying information.
- Replaced the ochre CTA with the common dark conclusion.

## Work

- Gave the hero one dominant Changan event image and two supports; evidence now outweighs the headline.
- Tightened featured-project spacing and aligned all compositions to the 12-column grid.
- Replaced the weak aerial brand-film frame with a stronger production context.
- Reduced the supporting grid from five to four projects to remove an orphaned weak card and avoid automotive over-representation.

## About

- Removed the people image that could be read as a team photograph.
- Used an approved robotics environment as the opening evidence and an approved panel context in the cross-border chapter.
- Removed the repeated panel image from the delivery chapter and let the method become typographic evidence.
- Retained one detailed three-step method and changed the conclusion to near-black.

## Contact and cases

- Contact retains the same low-friction form, with the refined palette, typography, buttons and footer scale.
- Case-study architecture and evidence are unchanged. Fact cards were converted into a ruled metadata plane and global type/media systems were applied.

## Media change count

- Images replaced in major roles: 2 (Home lead; Work brand-film frame).
- Images removed from public major-page compositions: 8 (three Creator supports, two Production outputs, one Industry moment, one Work supporting project, one repeated About image).
- Images newly surfaced: 0; all selections came from the already approved public registry.
