# Venus Bridge Media — Sitewide Commercial Copy & Editorial UX Audit

Date: 22 August 2026  
Scope: Home, For Companies, For Partners, Case Studies, all nine published case pages, How We Work, Contact, header, footer, CTAs and metadata in English and Chinese.

## Guardrails and outcome

The positioning remains **Cross-Border Collaboration & Market Presence Partner**. The work did not redesign the site, change URLs, alter the Case Evidence System, add service packages or expand any verified claim. Photography, film, events, talent and production remain project methods rather than the company category.

The editorial outcome is a clearer progression:

1. Venus Bridge turns a UK or European business need into a workable local project.
2. It helps choose the action, organises the participants and manages local execution.
3. It can be used for market presence, industry participation, institutional and talent engagement, employer branding and international brand evidence.
4. The client receives one coordination point rather than several disconnected suppliers.
5. Published case studies show what actually happened and what Venus Bridge handled.
6. The next step is to discuss one concrete need or introduce an organisation.

“Objective”, “context”, “credible”, “relevant”, “accountable”, “evidence”, “delivery”, “orchestration” and “commercial” remain where they add precision, but they no longer act as default filler. A repo-wide directional count across public TypeScript sources changed as follows: objective 173→156, context 150→129, credible 28→24, relevant 48→37, accountable 18→12, evidence 262→248, delivery 158→152, orchestration 3→2 and commercial 257→246. These totals include legacy surfaces, so they are a conservative measure of the reduction on canonical pages.

The Chinese canonical pages contain none of the prohibited phrases: 赋能、生态、全链路、一站式、全球化解决方案、资源整合平台、高校资源.

## Case-study and evidence review

All nine published cases were reviewed in English and Chinese. Project titles, client disclosure, dates, locations, roles, scope boundaries and approval state remain unchanged. The editorial layer only changes visitor-facing labels and navigation language. The following controls remain intact:

- published-only route and sitemap generation;
- verified evidence and rights gates;
- client-name disclosure rules;
- numbered archive rows and commercial filters;
- stable desktop preview and no preview on mobile;
- institutional and talent empty state until a record passes publication checks;
- maximum eight approved gallery items;
- related cases drawn only from the canonical published collection;
- “Discuss a similar objective” case CTA.

## Editorial typography and image balance

The visual system, section count, image selection, crop rules and image density remain unchanged. Copy length is similar or slightly reduced. The only typographic change is readability-related: homepage proof metadata and the hero scene number moved from 10px to 12px; Case Index category metadata moved to 11px, location to 14px and preview category to 12px. Divider, row and preview geometry remain unchanged.

The retained image rhythm supports the message ladder:

- the homepage hero carries one primary visual environment;
- selected proof shows three pieces of immediate evidence;
- the full Case Index remains text-led with one stable desktop preview;
- audience heroes use one relevant image rather than galleries;
- How We Work uses two contextual images before moving into responsibility and team structure;
- case pages keep a hero plus approved visual record, subordinate to the written evidence structure.

No additional cards, shadows, gradients, carousels or image-heavy portfolio treatments were introduced.

## Five first-time visitor simulations

### 1. Chinese founder planning a first UK market action

- Initial understanding: Venus Bridge will help decide what the local project should be and manage it, rather than sell a predefined event or film package.
- Previous ambiguity addressed: “context” and “credible project” did not say what happened next.
- Current route: For Companies → common outcome → suitable environment → Discuss a Project.
- Remaining human question: budget and lead-time expectations require a real conversation and are intentionally not invented.

### 2. International CMO needing evidence of European activity

- Initial understanding: the business can use a launch, industry moment or content project to create visible market participation and reusable material.
- Previous ambiguity addressed: proof sections could read as a production portfolio.
- Current route: homepage proof → Case Studies → Brand Evidence or Market Presence filter → similar-objective CTA.
- Remaining human question: post-project performance metrics are not claimed where evidence does not exist.

### 3. UK university or research contact

- Initial understanding: the page is an invitation to introduce an organisation, not a claim that it is already a partner.
- Previous ambiguity addressed: “Partners” could imply a published partner roster.
- Current route: For Partners → collaborator types → participation conditions → organisation intake.
- Remaining human question: named institutional relationships remain unpublished until evidence and permission pass all gates.

### 4. Venue, creator or production specialist

- Initial understanding: Venus Bridge brings a defined brief and coordinates approvals; the specialist is engaged for a specific fit and is not presented as staff.
- Previous ambiguity addressed: generic “partner value” did not immediately explain the practical benefit.
- Current route: For Partners → what partners receive → collaboration process → Introduce Your Organisation.
- Remaining human question: fees, contracting and usage terms are project-specific.

### 5. CEO comparing Venus Bridge with separate local suppliers

- Initial understanding: the differentiator is judgement plus one accountable coordination structure, not an unlimited consultancy claim.
- Previous ambiguity addressed: several sections repeated “accountable delivery” without stating the alternative.
- Current route: homepage operating model → “not ten disconnected suppliers” → How We Work responsibility → case evidence.
- Remaining human question: the precise boundary between Venus Bridge and each commissioned specialist is defined in the project scope.

The simulations did not reveal a remaining ambiguity that could be fixed safely with public copy alone. Unanswered questions concern commercial scope, evidence permission or project-specific terms and should remain part of a qualified conversation.

## QA record

Browser review covered the six required widths—1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430 and 375 pixels—across Home, For Companies, For Partners, Case Studies, How We Work and Contact in both languages: 72 main-page/viewport combinations. All nine published case pages were also reviewed in both languages at 1440 and 375 pixels: 36 case/viewport combinations.

Checks included reading order, navigation wrapping, heading line balance, horizontal overflow, minimum visible text size, image loading, Case Index row height, filters, empty state, desktop preview, mobile preview removal, CTA visibility, mobile menu vocabulary and footer consistency.

Results:

- no horizontal overflow;
- no broken public images;
- no detected heading orphan below the audit threshold;
- no visible canonical text below 11px after correction;
- desktop navigation remains single-line at 1280 and 1440;
- mobile menu contains the same conceptual navigation as header and footer;
- Brand Evidence returns five published rows;
- Institutional & Talent Engagement remains truthfully empty;
- desktop preview remains visible and mobile preview remains hidden;
- all case pages retain responsibility, public-record boundary, visual record and related-case sections.

Automated regression coverage is recorded in `e2e/editorial-copy-ux.spec.ts` in addition to the existing content, accessibility, motion, portfolio and release suites.

## Remaining human evidence required

- approval to publish any named institutional or university relationship;
- verified outcome metrics or client-approved testimonials for individual cases;
- confirmed public team names, roles, biographies, genuine photographs and permissions;
- project-specific commercial parameters such as timing, budget and contracting terms;
- any approved evidence that connects created assets to later commercial performance.
