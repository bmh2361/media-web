# Venus Bridge Media — Roadshow Production Scenarios

All four records are illustrative Production Scenarios (`status: concept`, `publicStatus: scenario`). They are not client work and cannot automatically become case studies.

## 1. UK Brand Launch Roadshow

For a first UK launch, multi-city meetings, product presentation and brand experience. Scope can include route and venue planning, guest communications, presenters, talent, suppliers, bilingual live production, photography, film, interviews and post-event handoff. Claims, rights and venue gates must be approved.

## 2. Investor & Strategic Partner Roadshow Production

For B2B capability presentation and professionally reviewed stakeholder meetings. Venus Bridge Media provides meeting/event production, bilingual materials, venue, scheduling and media production—not investment advice, solicitation, underwriting, securities sales or financial-promotion approval.

Potential financial promotions are `regulated-review-required`. The `financial-promotion` gate begins `blocked` and blocks both production release and campaign release. Invitations, investment materials and social content remain unpublished until the client’s lawyer and an appropriately FCA-authorised or permitted specialist confirm the route.

## 3. Product Demonstration & Buyer Roadshow

For technology, AI, automotive, fashion/beauty/apparel, B2B and consumer products. Scope includes demonstration design, sample logistics, buyer events, technical interviews, multi-city execution and follow-up assets. Product and claims gates cover safety information, performance/health/comparative claims, pricing and client approval.

## 4. Innovation, University & Industry Roadshow

For reviewed research communication, technology demonstration and industry exchange. Scope includes expert/guest coordination, bilingual technical translation, venues, interviews and media. It never implies a university relationship, institutional representation, technical endorsement or guaranteed research/investment outcome.

## Promotion rule

A scenario may become a case study only after real completion, evidence, client disclosure permission, media rights, legal approval and the existing case-publication gate all pass.
