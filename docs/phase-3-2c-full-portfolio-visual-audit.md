# Phase 3.2C Full Portfolio Visual Intelligence Audit

Date: 2026-08-13  
Source boundary: `public/` only  
Production UI status at completion of this document: unchanged

## Audit method

The audit inspected every image file under `public/`, then reviewed the highest-fidelity representative of each photographic moment rather than scoring responsive derivatives as if they were different photographs.

- 1,073 public image files were technically inventoried.
- 107 source photographs were visually reviewed: 83 art-directed desktop masters and 24 expertise originals.
- Sixteen expertise originals already have art-directed equivalents, leaving 91 distinct photographic moments.
- 67 portfolio masters belong to nine public project records.
- 24 expertise originals are capability-ready rather than case-ready.
- 21 brand files represent 12 logo/lockup families and remain interface assets.
- 52 demo files and five placeholders are not commercial evidence.
- Two legacy Framebridge scaffold images are unrelated to the Venus Bridge portfolio.
- `public/media.zip` is a byte-equivalent archive of the existing `public/media/` tree, not an additional evidence library.

The machine-readable lineage, dimensions, formats, hashes and family counts are in `audit/phase-3-2c/pre-implementation/public-media-inventory.json`. Eight labelled contact sheets record the visual review set.

## Scoring convention

Each coherent series is scored from 1–10 for:

- CC — Commercial Context
- ES — Evidence Strength
- VQ — Visual Quality
- SF — Strategic Fit
- DI — Differentiation
- BR — Buyer Relevance
- CR — Crop Resilience
- BA — Brand Authority
- HI — Human Interest
- OR — Overuse Risk, where 1 is low risk and 10 is high risk

Human judgement overrides the arithmetic. A high-quality image may remain supporting-only when its role is ambiguous, its context is weak or repeated use would distort the business category.

## Commercial balance conclusion

- Automotive remains the strongest named case category but should stop dominating Home and Capabilities.
- Technology/AI/robotics is visually credible enough for high-level capability presentation, but must remain capability media rather than a named case.
- Creator/product/fashion media can materially improve buyer relevance when framed through activation, casting and outputs.
- Institutional/expert capability should remain text-first until permissioned case metadata exists.
- The right redesign uses fewer repeated automotive images and four newly surfaced creator assets; it does not require publishing every approved photo.
