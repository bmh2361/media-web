# Venus Bridge Media — UK Market Entry Scope

Last reviewed: 17 July 2026  
Next review: 17 October 2026  
Public default: `coordination`

## Positioning

UK Market Entry & Compliance Coordination connects practical UK setup preparation with bilingual communications, content production, launches, roadshows and local delivery. It is not a legal, tax, accounting, investment or company-formation agency service.

## Venus Bridge Media can directly provide

- Requirements interviews, stakeholder maps and bilingual information checklists.
- Project plans, decision logs, responsibility maps, timelines and approval trackers.
- Claims inventories, evidence registers, rights trackers and multilingual consistency checks.
- Brand communications, websites, campaign content, photography, film and interviews.
- Event, exhibition, roadshow, venue, supplier, presenter and local-crew coordination.
- Structured asset and project handoff.

## Venus Bridge Media can coordinate

- Company-setup information preparation and specialist question lists.
- Accountant, tax adviser, customs agent, commercial solicitor and trade mark specialist briefs.
- VAT, EORI, payroll, insurance, banking and payment-provider workstream questions.
- Data, privacy, direct-marketing and advertising review routes.
- Product-category, label, claim, testing and responsible-party requirement mapping.
- Financial-promotion review routing before any investor-roadshow material is released.

Coordination does not mean Venus Bridge Media makes the professional determination or controls a third-party outcome.

## Referral only

- Legal, company-law, tax and accounting advice.
- Companies House identity verification and ACSP activity.
- Formal company filings and statutory agency services.
- Trade mark legal clearance, registration advice and disputes.
- Data-protection legal advice and formal regulatory interpretation.
- Product certification, conformity assessment and statutory Responsible Person services.
- Immigration, investment and regulated financial-promotion advice or approval.

## Explicitly not provided

- Registration, bank-account, payment-account, trade mark, product-certification, funding or market-access guarantees.
- Audit, regulated AML/KYC, immigration or FCA approval.
- Claims that a campaign is “fully compliant” or “legally approved by us”.
- Collection of identity, banking or unencrypted KYC files through the public contact form.

## Future expansion conditions

`partnered` mode remains unavailable until every public partner has verified qualifications, register evidence, insurance, written scope, referral and conflict procedures, approved data-sharing terms, logo permission, approved public wording and an independently reviewed responsibility boundary.
