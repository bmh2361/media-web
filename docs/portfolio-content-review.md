## Classification rationale

The three Selected Projects have source-supported public project identity, location and year. The remaining real records stay under Production Experience because their full brief, exact delivery scope or date is not available. Production Scenarios remain a separate content system and are never used as evidence for these records.

The six anonymous talent-category images are page-level capability examples rather than portfolio projects. Contact details, CV pages, measurements, rates and private social information from the talent PDF were not imported.
