# Phase 3.1 Owner Evidence Pipeline

Date: 12 August 2026  
Purpose: evidence intake SOP for every future public case, capability claim or named relationship.

## Intake rule

No project enters public copy because it is commercially attractive. It enters only when the owner can document identity, scope, Venus Bridge’s exact role, rights, permissions and approved wording. Attendance is not participation; participation is not partnership; engagement is not endorsement; commissioned work is not independent recommendation.

Every intake should produce:

1. a completed evidence record;
2. source files or links with a review date;
3. an approved public role statement;
4. media/publication permission;
5. a claim level (A–D) under `phase-3-1-proof-matrix.md`;
6. a named owner responsible for final approval.

## Institutional / Academic Projects

Collect:

- Institution’s exact legal/public name.
- Participant identity, public title and department where relevant.
- Exact relationship: introduction, visit, engagement, commissioned discussion, sponsorship, research activity, formal partnership or another documented type.
- Event/project name, location and date.
- Venus Bridge’s exact role and responsibilities.
- Institution and participant permission for public reference.
- Approved photographs and video with rights owner, permitted territories/channels and expiry.
- Exact approved public wording in English and Chinese.
- Separate logo permission; never infer it from name permission.
- Public/private/confidential status and embargo date.
- Written confirmation of whether endorsement language is prohibited or explicitly authorised.

Minimum publication gate: identity + relationship + Venus role + public wording + media/name permissions. Without all five, keep the record internal and unnamed.

## Expert Projects

Collect:

- Expert identity and current approved professional title.
- Relevant expertise and the source supporting it.
- Engagement type: paid/commissioned, invited, interview, roundtable, advisory, presentation or another precise form.
- Participant consent for name, title, image, quotation and project association separately.
- Content and recording rights, permitted edits and usage channels.
- Approved English and Chinese title/biography.
- Approved claim describing the expert’s involvement.
- Explicit confirmation that involvement does or does not constitute endorsement.

Minimum publication gate: identity + engagement type + consent + rights + approved involvement wording.

## Exhibition / Event Projects

Collect:

- Event name, organiser, venue, city and dates.
- Client/brand identity and permission to name it.
- Venus Bridge role, separated into strategy, coordination, staffing, production, photography, film and handoff as applicable.
- Event organiser/venue rules for commercial publication.
- Photography/video rights and any attendee-release constraints.
- Confirmed outputs and quantities.
- Approved short public description in English and Chinese.
- Measurable outcomes only when the source, method and client approval are recorded.

Minimum publication gate: event + client permission + exact Venus role + event/media rights + approved wording.

## Creator / Talent Projects

Collect:

- Talent/creator identity or approved anonymous classification.
- Paid, commissioned, gifted, sponsored or other exact relationship.
- Contracted role and production dates.
- Image, performance and recording rights.
- Brand usage rights by territory, channel, duration and paid/organic media.
- Social usage, handle/tagging and whitelisting rights where applicable.
- Approved English and Chinese wording.
- Confirmation that commissioned participation is not presented as independent endorsement.

Minimum publication gate: relationship + contracted role + image/performance rights + brand/social usage + approved wording.

## Creative Production

Collect:

- Client/brand identity and permission status.
- Project title, date and location.
- Exact production scope: direction, photography, film, crew, casting, styling, locations, post-production and delivery.
- Final deliverables and approved examples.
- Copyright owner, licence, territories, channels, duration and third-party restrictions.
- Publication rights for the Venus Bridge website, social and credentials materials separately.
- Approved project description and role labels in English and Chinese.
- Measurable outcomes only when available, attributable and approved.

Minimum publication gate: project identity + exact production role + deliverables + publication rights + approved wording.

## Evidence strength decision

- Level A only when the public case directly proves the exact claim.
- Level B when it proves important components but not complete ownership.
- Level C when the owner confirms current deliverability but no suitable public case exists; publish unnamed, conditional formats only.
- Level D remains internal and is not published as current capability.

## Final owner checklist for each case

- [ ] Client/participant identity approved.
- [ ] Venus Bridge role narrowed and approved.
- [ ] Relationship type accurate.
- [ ] English wording approved.
- [ ] Chinese wording approved independently.
- [ ] Name/logo permissions separated.
- [ ] Image/video/content rights recorded.
- [ ] Outputs supported.
- [ ] Metrics supported or removed.
- [ ] Endorsement implication reviewed.
- [ ] Public/private/embargo status recorded.
- [ ] Evidence level assigned.
- [ ] Review/expiry date assigned.
- [ ] Final owner/legal approval recorded.
