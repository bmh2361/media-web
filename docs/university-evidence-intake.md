# University, Institutional & Talent Evidence Intake

Use this intake before changing any public institutional case from text-led, publication-limited evidence to a named case with media. One record is required per programme. Unknown fields remain blank; they must never be inferred from photographs, venue geography or informal participation.

## Required case fields

- Public case title
- Exact organisation name
- Relationship type: official university / department / institute / student society / student organisation / university community / venue only / other
- Institutional relationship level: A official institution; B society, organisation or affiliated community; C location only
- Client
- Sponsor and exact sponsorship direction
- Date
- City and venue
- Commercial objective
- Target audience
- Venus Bridge role and responsibility boundary
- Collaborators and their exact roles
- Programme format
- Deliverables
- Measurable evidence supported by a source
- Reusable assets or relationships supported by a source
- Media filenames and captions
- Media rights owner and website-use permission
- Logo permission
- Organisation naming permission
- Participant naming and image permission
- Public-link evidence
- Confidentiality restrictions
- Final approved English wording
- Final approved Chinese wording
- Evidence reviewer and review date

## Publication gates

1. A Cambridge or London location does not establish a university relationship.
2. A student society or community programme must not be described as an official university partnership.
3. Sponsor, client, organiser and venue are separate roles and must be recorded separately.
4. University logos remain unpublished without explicit usage permission.
5. Attendance, recruitment outcomes, reach, ROI and relationship outcomes require a citable source.
6. Media is added to `content/commercial-evidence.ts` only after rights, naming and relationship checks pass; then set `mediaStatus` to `ready`.
7. If evidence remains incomplete, retain the current text-led composition or suppress the case. Never render an empty media placeholder.
