## Evidence level decision

- **Level A — Public Verified:** real engagement, exact role, complete public permission and approved wording. Eligible for a public case.
- **Level B — Verified but Non-Public:** real relationship/engagement without complete public permission. Internal sales evidence only.
- **Level C — Access / Network Capability:** relevant pathway exists without project-specific public agreement. Cautious capability wording only.

No record may become Level A without permission evidence, an exact role, a scope boundary and at least one verifiable activity/output record.
