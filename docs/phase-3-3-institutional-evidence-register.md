## Publication decision

- Keep institutional, university, research and expert collaboration as a real but conditional capability.

- Do not publish “official partner”, “university partner”, endorsement language, institution logos or named relationship tiles.

## HUMAN DATA REQUIRED

For each proposed institutional proof item, the owner must supply: project name, contracting party, institution/department, date and place, objective, exact Venus Bridge responsibility, final outputs, delivery/publication evidence, approved bilingual wording, public-name permission and separate logo permission.
