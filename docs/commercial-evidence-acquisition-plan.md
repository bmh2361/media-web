# Commercial evidence acquisition plan

This is an operating plan, not proof of outcomes. Existing visual-documentation cases remain unchanged and are not assigned new commercial evidence classes.

Priority intake:

1. One permissioned `MARKET_VALIDATION` case with the decision, excluded scope and verified result.
2. One `BUYER_DISTRIBUTOR_ENGAGEMENT` or `PARTNER_ENGAGEMENT` case with counterparty type, activity boundary and approved continued value.
3. One `EXHIBITION_FULL_CYCLE` case covering before, on-site and after work without inferring commercial results.
4. One approved UK/European partner-side quote with exact relationship context and publication permission.
5. One measurable, permission-safe commercial output with evidence source, metric definition and verification date.

For every intake, complete the typed fields in `FutureCommercialCaseIntake`: starting situation, decision, objective, geography, scope, did/did not do, participant types, three-stage activity, outputs, verified result, continued value, optional approved metric/quotes, evidence source, publication/image rights, legal review and last verification date. Claims remain private until rights and owner/legal gates pass.
