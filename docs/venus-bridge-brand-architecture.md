# Venus Bridge brand architecture

## Master brand

**VENUS BRIDGE** is the public-facing name prospects should remember. In normal English and Chinese prose, use `Venus Bridge`.

## Descriptor

**GLOBAL PARTNERSHIPS** is a brand descriptor, not part of the legal company name. It belongs primarily in the approved logo lockup, footer identity, social identity and selected brand-introduction contexts. Do not repeat `Venus Bridge Global Partnerships` as if it were a long company name.

## Commercial positioning

Venus Bridge helps Chinese companies build relevant market relationships, credible presence and accountable local execution across the UK and Europe.

The proposition should remain concrete: market validation, buyer and distributor engagement, commercial partnerships, launches, exhibitions, local operating capacity and follow-through.

## Legal identity

Legal identity is configuration-driven and is not equal to the descriptor.

- `content/company.ts` remains the source for the approved legal entity, trading identity, privacy controller, company number, registered office and legal approval state.
- `GLOBAL PARTNERSHIPS` must never be used as `legalName` unless an owner-approved legal change is supplied.
- Privacy, Terms, consent and regulated-scope wording must continue to use the approved configuration or current legally reviewed trading identity.

## Correct usage

- Logo: **VENUS BRIDGE / GLOBAL PARTNERSHIPS**
- Body copy: `Venus Bridge helps Chinese companies…`
- SEO: `Venus Bridge` plus an explicit market proposition
- Case attribution: `Venus Bridge`
- Footer brand description: a concise market-relationship and local-execution proposition
- Legal layer: the approved configuration-driven entity or trading identity
- Capability: `Creative & Media Production` where media is genuinely part of delivery

## Incorrect usage

- `Venus Bridge Global Partnerships` as a legal or long-form company name
- `Venus Bridge Media` as the current general public master brand
- `Global Partnerships` as the only explanation of what clients buy
- Claims of a global network, exclusive access, guaranteed introductions or universal institutional relationships
- Descriptions that reduce Venus Bridge to a resource platform, broker, event agency or media company

- Desktop Header: `webp/venus-bridge-horizontal-lockup-white.webp`
- Mobile Header: `webp/venus-bridge-monogram-white.webp`
- Footer: `webp/venus-bridge-horizontal-lockup-white.webp`
- Opening identity: `webp/venus-bridge-monogram-white.webp`
- OG cards: restrained `png/venus-bridge-horizontal-lockup-white.png`
- Favicon and application icons: approved monogram ICO/PNG set
- Light surfaces: black equivalents only
- Canonical asset root: `public/brand/venus-bridge/`

Do not crop, stretch, recolour, auto-trace or apply visual effects to supplied logo artwork.

## Media terminology

Media is a capability, not the company category. Retain the term where it describes photography, film, interviews, event documentation, paid or social media, launch assets or other genuine outputs. The hierarchy is commercial objective, market and relationship strategy, local execution, then creative or media production where required.

## Chinese usage

- Use `Venus Bridge` or `VENUS BRIDGE` in normal Chinese prose.
- The approved logo may retain the English descriptor `GLOBAL PARTNERSHIPS`.
- Prefer natural terms such as `跨境商业合作`, `英国与欧洲市场`, `买家与经销商`, `行业关系`, `市场验证`, `本地执行`, `展会与发布` and `商务跟进`.
- Use `全球合作` selectively; do not translate the descriptor repeatedly or mechanically.
- Avoid `资源对接平台`, `海外资源整合平台` and wording that implies brokerage or guaranteed access.
