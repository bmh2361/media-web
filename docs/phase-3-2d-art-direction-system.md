# Phase 3.2D art-direction system

## Creative position

Venus Bridge is presented as an international B2B operating partner with creative-production fluency. The visual system sits at the intersection of four modes:

- editorial: decisive type, selective media, controlled asymmetry;
- commercial: immediate service clarity, legible evidence and visible next steps;
- production: human and event energy at meaningful scale;
- institutional/technology: precise information, restrained colour and calm interaction.

It must not resolve into a photography portfolio, talent agency, management-consultancy template, SaaS dashboard, hospitality palette or generic luxury theme.

## Governing principle: controlled contrast

Different project worlds retain their own visual energy. Coherence comes from the same grid, type hierarchy, tonal palette, caption grammar and transition timing—not from forcing every asset into the same module.

The recurring sequence is:

1. proposition;
2. evidence at authority scale;
3. precise context;
4. a restrained route forward.

## Baseline diagnosis

The Phase 3.2D baseline contains no broken system-level functionality, but its composition has eight priority weaknesses:

1. the homepage hero separates a narrow text tower from a phone-like media frame;
2. dark/light sections alternate mechanically and finish in a large ochre block;
3. the homepage and Capabilities pages repeat multi-image collage grammar;
4. Capabilities treats project worlds as interface cards before showing evidence;
5. Work gives its headline more authority than its opening evidence;
6. About repeats large statement/small support compositions and uses ambiguous people imagery;
7. body, metadata and process text are too small relative to available canvas;
8. mobile often retains too many small media moments instead of making fewer images authoritative.

## Page personalities

### Home — composed overview

Fast proposition, broad landscape hero, tactile outcomes, one active capability field, selected work with one dominant project, concise process, clear differentiators, dark concluding conversation.

### Capabilities — four project worlds

Quiet typographic authority for institutions; cinematic scale for industry; campaign restraint for creators; output-led sequencing for production. One controlled mosaic maximum.

### Work — publication

Evidence leads. Featured projects share one underlying grid but vary by landscape, portrait and full-width editorial roles. Supporting work uses two ratios only.

### About — human credibility

Purpose, cross-border context and working method. Avoid any image that could imply an unverified team or relationship. Real project environments carry credibility.

### Contact — ease

Short, calm and operational. The form is the focal point and the route to starting is obvious.

### Case studies — proof

Context, Venus role, delivered outputs and media must read as one evidence sequence. Hero and gallery frames respond to the project’s strongest source composition.

## Composition rules

- Every section has one primary focal point and no more than two supporting focal points.
- One image must dominate every multi-image composition.
- A mosaic is a special event, never the default container.
- Media can exceed reading measure and approach the editorial grid edge.
- Empty space must separate ideas, create anticipation or protect a focal point; otherwise it is removed.
- Captions explain context or role. They do not merely name what is visible.
- Accent colour appears as a small signal: logo, eyebrow, rule, index or interaction state.
- Interaction is slow, low-amplitude and optional; static and reduced-motion states remain complete.

## Responsive intent

- 1440/1280: use the full editorial canvas; avoid narrow centred islands.
- 1024: become an intentional six-column composition, not compressed desktop.
- 768: use strong two-part relationships or a single dominant flow.
- 390: fewer, larger images; concise type; metadata remains readable; no miniature mosaics.

## Acceptance test

At every audited width a visitor must first understand what Venus Bridge makes possible, then see credible evidence, then find an obvious conversation path. Visual variety must increase attention without weakening that sequence.
