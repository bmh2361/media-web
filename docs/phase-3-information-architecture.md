## Homepage architecture

1. Hero — category, audience, commercial result, UK context, two CTAs.
2. Early Proof — verified execution proof immediately after the hero.
3. What We Help You Achieve — four customer outcomes.
4. Capabilities — four pillars, concise scope and evidence-led media.
5. Selected Work — real project evidence with narrow role labels.
6. How a Project Comes Together — Define the Outcome; Build the Right UK Network; Deliver & Document.
7. Why Venus Bridge — supported differentiators only.
8. Final CTA — one low-friction commercial invitation.

## Capabilities page

1. Hero
2. Four-capability overview
3. Institutional & Expert Collaboration
4. Industry Presence & Events
5. Creators, Talent & Cultural Partnerships
6. Creative Production & Brand Assets
7. Example programme combinations
8. Selected relevant work
9. CTA

Each capability explains the business problem, possible engagement formats, Venus Bridge’s coordination role and client outputs. Lists illustrate programme components; they are not a catalogue or rate card.

## Work architecture

1. Hero and truth-gate explanation
2. Execution cases
3. Capability evidence
4. CTA

Each case detail follows: Context → Venus Bridge Role → What Was Delivered → Outputs → Capabilities Demonstrated → Media → CTA. No KPI appears unless verified.

## About architecture

1. Why Venus Bridge exists
2. The fragmented UK environment it coordinates
3. Supported operating strengths: London base, bilingual communication, local execution and production experience
4. Three-step working model
5. Claim boundary and CTA

## Contact architecture

One concise invitation plus a short form: Name, Company, Role, Email or WeChat, objective, optional timing and optional support type. The user is not required to diagnose their own service need.

## Footer

Capabilities, Work, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and London, UK. No duplicate sitemap or retired taxonomy.
