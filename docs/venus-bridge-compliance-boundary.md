# Venus Bridge Media — Compliance Responsibility Boundary

This document is an operating boundary, not legal advice. Public claims must stay within `content/market-entry.ts` and the release validator.

| Area                   | Venus Bridge Media role                                 | Qualified specialist/client role                                                     | Public boundary                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Legal                  | Organise questions, documents, meetings and approvals   | Solicitor gives legal advice and drafts/reviews legal documents                      | Never describe VBM as legal counsel                    |
| Tax and accounting     | Map workstreams and prepare referral briefs             | Accountant/tax adviser determines VAT, tax, payroll and accounting treatment         | No tax conclusions or accounting practice              |
| Company setup          | Prepare information and project timeline                | Client or provider chooses structure and files                                       | No registration guarantee or authorised-agent claim    |
| ACSP and identity      | Identify need and arrange a secure handoff              | Registered ACSP or Companies House handles verification                              | No identity documents in public form                   |
| Trade marks and IP     | Public-search assistance, asset list and referral brief | Qualified IP professional gives clearance, filing and dispute advice                 | No formal clearance or registration promise            |
| Data and privacy       | Map data touchpoints and review route                   | Client/controller and data specialist determine lawful processing                    | No UK GDPR or PECR legal opinion                       |
| Advertising and claims | Claims inventory, evidence matrix and approval tracker  | Client and appointed reviewer approve claims                                         | No ASA pre-approval or complaint guarantee             |
| Products               | Category map, document list and specialist referral     | Manufacturer/importer/responsible party and product specialist determine obligations | No certification, certificate or market-access promise |
| Financial promotions   | Produce only after lawful route is approved             | Client lawyer and appropriately authorised/permitted specialist decide and approve   | Potential promotion remains blocked                    |
| Immigration            | Record that a need exists                               | Regulated immigration adviser provides advice                                        | No immigration advice                                  |
| Banking/payments       | Organise application-information checklist              | Bank/payment provider decides; adviser supports if appropriate                       | No account-opening guarantee                           |
| Insurance              | Classify cover questions and introduce a broker         | Broker/insurer advises and underwrites                                               | No coverage recommendation or outcome promise          |

## Responsibility layers

Venus Bridge Media owns bilingual coordination, production planning, evidence and rights workflow, content/event production and handoff. Independent specialists own regulated professional opinions. The client owns submitted information, appointments, final legal/commercial decisions, filings, claims approval, product safety and continuing statutory obligations.

## Secure information rule

The public form may collect business context but never passports, identity cards, bank details, sensitive shareholder records or KYC evidence. Any later transfer requires a separately approved secure system, purpose definition, access control and retention policy.
