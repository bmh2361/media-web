## Language rules applied

- “Proof”, “evidence”, “verified”, “capability-ready”, evidence tiers and maturity-state names do not lead client-facing sections.
- Precise role statements remain on project pages.
- English avoids agency superlatives, consultancy jargon and generic luxury language.
- Chinese is authored as direct commercial Chinese rather than mirroring English word order.
- Confidentiality appears once in the institutional chapter and nowhere else unless a real confidential engagement is published.
