# Team portrait guidelines

## Source standard

- Orientation: portrait
- Recommended ratio: 4:5
- Minimum resolution: 1600 × 2000px
- Preferred background: neutral white, light grey or warm neutral
- Subject: front-facing or at a very slight angle
- Crop: head and shoulders or upper torso
- Eye line: approximately 35–42% from the top
- Lighting: soft, professional and neutral

Avoid strong coloured or busy backgrounds, full-body photographs, event photographs, extreme angles, heavy beauty filters and low-resolution screenshots.

## Website treatment

Every portrait is rendered in the same 4:5 frame with `object-fit: cover`. The `focalPoint` stored for each person controls the individual `object-position`; new members must be visually checked and assigned their own focal point rather than inheriting a universal crop. Do not stretch, automatically replace backgrounds or apply heavy colour filters.
