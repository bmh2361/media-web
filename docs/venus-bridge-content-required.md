## 8. Candidate-specific questions

### Artist concert imagery

- Was Venus Bridge Media the official media team, an appointed supplier, or another role?
- Which artist/event/date may be named?
- Who owns the photo/video copyright?
- Is music audible in any clip?
- Are audience, backstage and artist images separately approved?

### Founder/brand interview

- Was the project completed and delivered?
- Was two-camera 4K capture and lavalier audio actually used?
- Did Venus Bridge Media edit the long version and short cutdowns?
- What was the final use and where was it published?

### Automotive/Goodwood direction

- Which item was delivered, which is in progress and which was only quoted?
- May the event or vehicle brand be named?
- Was hosting, livestream, filming, logistics or client hospitality actually in scope?
- Are event, brand and presenter media approved?

### Fashion, beauty and apparel

- Was the project for a B2B brand, an artist, or a consumer client?
- Which product/collection and channels were involved?
- Who supplied models, makeup, hair and wardrobe?
- Are model, stylist, brand and location rights complete?

### Agency/local execution

- May the project be described anonymously?
- What client-facing relationship must remain confidential?
- Which UK suppliers and responsibilities were coordinated?
- What handoff documents or assets can be shown without revealing the client?
