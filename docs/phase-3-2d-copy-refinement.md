# Phase 3.2D copy refinement

## Objective

Reduce repeated framework language and let visual evidence carry more of the explanation. Strategic positioning, four capabilities, four project types, evidence claims and CTA destinations remain unchanged.

## Editing rules

- Prefer concrete verbs and project contexts to abstract nouns.
- Avoid repeating “objective”, “proof”, “system”, “framework”, “execution” and “credibility” in adjacent sections.
- Home retains the brief operating story; About owns the detailed method.
- Capability descriptions explain what a client could build, not an internal department.
- Work headlines lead quickly to evidence.
- Chinese is edited independently for commercial rhythm, not translated word-for-word.
- No new claims or implied relationships are introduced.

## Planned reductions

- Home: shorten section support text, project metadata and the three-step explanations.
- Capabilities: remove repeated explanatory captions and reduce accordion-like route detail.
- Work: shorten the opening and featured-project support lines.
- About: remove duplicated operating-model explanations and keep one detailed delivery section.
- Contact: retain the current low-friction instruction and form labels.

The final audit will record the exact changed-string count and estimated public-copy reduction.
