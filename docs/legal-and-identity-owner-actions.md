- legal entity mode: pre-incorporation or incorporated;
- exact legal company name, company number and registered office where applicable;
- exact privacy controller;
- exact approved EN and ZH trading-name statement;
- approved public business and privacy contacts;
- Privacy and Terms effective dates;
- confirmation that company facts may be published;
- independent legal-review confirmation.

The unresolved wording is whether Venus Bridge is a trading name, a business brand operated by an entity, or another legally correct relationship. Configure only the wording counsel approves. Privacy, Terms, Footer, Contact and Organization schema consume `content/company.ts`; missing values keep approval and schema pending. Translation correction is not legal approval.
