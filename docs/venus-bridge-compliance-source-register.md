## Governance

- Source records include `lastCheckedAt`, `nextReviewAt` and status.
- Expired or `review-required` sources produce a staging warning and block a public production release.
- A source update does not automatically change public copy. Content changes require scope review, bilingual review and, where appropriate, independent legal review.
- Blogs, forums, registration-agent marketing pages, social posts and AI-generated legal summaries are not acceptable sources.
