# Venus Bridge final pre-launch commercial audit

## Commercial verdict

The site now clearly sells a **China-to-UK/Europe market execution and local delivery layer**, not a list of creative services. A cold buyer can identify audience, geography, buying situations, operating benefit, real project activity and next step within 10–20 seconds. The commercial architecture is ready; the proof system is not yet as broad as the proposition.

If active outbound begins tomorrow: **NO, until the P0 release/contact/legal/security/social-card gates close.** After those gates and the selected measurement/contact P1s, the site is credible enough to receive serious prospects. Do not delay launch to invent stronger case outcomes.

## Procurement readiness

Marketing and overseas-business leaders can understand what they may buy: a defined local programme assembled around a market goal. Procurement/finance will still need a proposal specifying legal entity, scope, owners, deliverables, third parties, rights, timing, fees, success criteria and data handling. The website correctly does not try to replace that document.

Current procurement blockers are operational rather than architectural: incomplete legal identity/notices, no live contact verification, no public fallback, and no quantified/reference proof. Do **not** add pricing.

## Conversion map and friction

```text
Cold outreach/search/share
  → Home recognition + real project proof
    → Companies (problem/benefit fit) or relevant Case (evidence)
      → How We Work / About (delivery and team confidence)
        → Contact intent selection
          → qualified form
            → real provider success + sales follow-up
```

Strengths: proof is early; CTAs consistently route company/partner intent; no dead-end canonical page; case pages relate back; form asks enough to qualify without requiring a finished brief.

Friction: obsolete share previews distort the first touch; no public response expectation/fallback; the case library may lower confidence after the broad promise; analytics cannot show where drop-off occurs.

## Future content acquisition list

For every eligible project, agree evidence capture at contracting—not after delivery:

- commercial situation and baseline before engagement;
- market/geography and decision the client needed to make;
- exact Venus Bridge scope, responsibility owner and partner/supplier boundaries;
- project duration, key dates and delivery locations;
- target organisation/stakeholder types (not confidential names by default);
- buyer/distributor/partner meeting or qualified-conversation count where meaningful;
- stage progression: introduction → meeting → evaluation → next step;
- launch/exhibition attendance or relevant audience composition with source/method;
- deliverables and channels actually used;
- follow-up completed, market learning documented and assets reused;
- repeat engagement or expanded scope;
- approved client quote focused on speed, judgement, complexity or outcome;
- approved partner quote focused on context, coordination and reputation safety;
- approved client/partner logos and exact attribution language;
- before/after business context and measurable outcomes only where causally supportable;
- media rights, names, metrics, quote, legal and publication approval recorded separately.

## Highest-ROI commercial decisions

1. Fix the share card so outbound links communicate the same category as the page.
2. Make Contact operational, recoverable and measurable.
3. Freeze the positioning/navigation/Home/Companies architecture.
4. Treat proof acquisition as an operating process, not a copywriting exercise.
5. Let future commercially stronger cases push creative cases down the archive; do not delete current truthful work pre-launch.
