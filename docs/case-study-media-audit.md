# Case Study Media Audit

Completed against all 62 approved source records in the 12 active projects. The generated [source inventory](../audit/case-study-media-presentation/source-media-inventory.md) is the canonical filename, native-size, ratio, orientation and alt-text register. Twelve internal contact sheets are in `audit/case-study-media-presentation/contact-sheets/` and are not public site assets.

## Audit key

The image numbers below follow source order in the inventory. `F/P/B/E` means faces, product, branding and important edge content. `Y`, `N`, and `L` mean yes, no, and limited. Focal coordinates are normalised `x,y`. Every gallery placement uses natural ratio; `cover` is allowed only for the explicitly safe Changan preview. Quality reflects the safe display size, not photographic merit.

## Asset limitations

- The 952-pixel automotive event and film sources are deliberately capped at 1000px display width and are not used as fake full-bleed 1920px assets.
- The 711×771 beauty makeup frame is classified `LIMITED` and rendered as a small detail.
- Several event sources contain intentional original-frame edge content; these are preserved naturally rather than “fixed” with CSS cropping.
- No source path was renamed. Existing Unicode, spaces and AVIF paths were exercised by the screenshot suite without load failure.
