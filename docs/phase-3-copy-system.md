## Capability descriptions

### Institutional & Expert Collaboration / 高校、机构与专家合作

Design precise engagements with academics, researchers, technical experts and professional communities when the client objective calls for credible knowledge exchange. Venus Bridge coordinates the format, participants, logistics and documentation; it does not imply endorsement.

### Industry Presence & Events / 行业参与与活动执行

Help companies participate meaningfully in exhibitions, conferences, roundtables, visits and brand events, with local coordination and documentation matched to the commercial objective.

### Creators, Talent & Cultural Partnerships / 创作者、人才与文化合作

Coordinate creators, presenters, models, performers, stylists and cultural collaborators as part of a wider brand programme. Engagements are described accurately as commissioned or paid where relevant.

### Creative Production & Brand Assets / 创意制作与品牌资产

Convert UK activity into commercial photography, films, interviews, event coverage, social content and stakeholder material designed for continued use in China, Asia and international conversations.

## Terminology rules

- Use `connect`, `coordinate`, `design`, `execute`, `document`, `produce` and `deliver` only for the scope evidenced.
- Use `collaboration`, `engagement`, `participation`, `commissioned work`, `production support` and `event documentation` precisely.
- `Partner` refers to Venus Bridge’s client-facing operating role, not to an official institutional relationship.
- Name an institution, expert, creator, event or brand only when identity, relationship, permission and public wording are verified.
- Present talent and contacts as components within an orchestrated programme, never as a commodity catalogue.

## Forbidden or discouraged language

Do not publish: end-to-end market entry, one-stop expansion, full lifecycle internationalisation, ecosystem empowerment, seamless solution, university endorsement, international backing, official partner, strategic adviser, full campaign agency, or equivalent Chinese claims such as 一站式出海、全链路出海、出海闭环 and 全球资源赋能 without specific proof.

## Claim governance

Every significant claim is classified internally:

- **VERIFIED:** supported by a named source, permission or documented project record; may be published precisely.
- **SUPPORTED:** supported by current operating evidence but phrased without exclusivity or formal-status implications; may be published conservatively.
- **ASPIRATIONAL:** a future direction; not presented as a current capability.
- **NOT SAFE TO PUBLISH:** missing evidence, permission or precise scope; excluded from public copy.

Institutional endorsement, official partnership, client-agency status, campaign ownership, KPIs and regulatory capability default to NOT SAFE TO PUBLISH until documented.

## Translation principles

Chinese copy is written for founders, CEOs, CMOs, brand leaders and international business teams rather than translated sentence by sentence. Use commercially natural terms such as 英国本地合作、国际品牌可信度、行业参与、专家资源、海外品牌资产、本地执行 and 国际传播素材. Avoid inflated “出海” slogans and unexplained consulting frameworks. Both languages use concrete outputs and narrow, verifiable role descriptions.
