## Audit and implementation notes

### Commercial story

- The homepage now leads with the Chinese-company buyer and a concrete UK/European market move. Real approved project proof appears early; the partner journey remains present but secondary.
- Five recognisable sales triggers replace abstract objective language: market entry, launch or exhibition, buyers/distributors/partners, local credibility and limited local-team capacity.
- Four outcome-led solution groups replace a long service catalogue: market validation and entry; buyers, partners and industry presence; launches, exhibitions and local activation; credibility, content and market assets.
- The fractional local-team model is stated as market coordination, relationship advancement and execution capacity around a defined objective. It makes no employment, office or permanent-team claim.
- Exhibition work now follows a before / on-the-ground / after sequence, making meetings, delivery, assets and follow-through part of one commercial programme.

### Audience pages and conversion

- Companies now opens with market entry and growth through local commercial execution, then moves from buyer situations to outcomes, evidence, operating model and CTA.
- Partners now explains the opportunity, role, context, process and value without presenting capability categories as formal relationships.
- Contact qualifies by business situation rather than service taxonomy. Name, organisation, email, objective and consent are required; role, website and timing remain optional.
- CTA hierarchy is consistent: a company-project discussion is primary, case studies are secondary, and organisation introduction is partner-specific.

### Case-study truth and quality

- All 12 published portfolio records continue to derive from `content/portfolio.ts` and remain fail-closed behind evidence, rights, approval and publication gates.
- Each detail now presents: Project Objective, Business Context, Challenge, Venus Bridge Role, Strategy / Approach, Local Delivery, Outputs / Outcomes, and What Remained Useful.
- Scope language was strengthened only where supported by existing records. No sales result, buyer access, endorsement, office, institutional relationship, client ownership or quantified outcome was invented.
- The 8.5/10 case score is deliberate: the narratives are commercially clearer, but several records substantiate visual documentation or production rather than wider commercial ownership. The score will rise only when stronger approved evidence exists.

### English and Chinese

- English was rewritten in concise British B2B language around validate, enter, engage, launch, deliver and follow through.
- Chinese was authored for natural commercial comprehension rather than sentence-by-sentence translation. It uses buyer-facing terms such as 市场验证、商务对接、买家、经销商、本地执行、展会、发布 and 后续跟进.
- Defensive normal-page microcopy was removed. Required legal, consent and internal governance language remains intact.

### Media and visual-system preservation

- The black, ivory and restrained-gold system, typography, whitespace, motion, navigation, globe, case archive and editorial grids were preserved.
- Existing real project imagery remains the source of proof. No stock imagery, fake logo wall, testimonial, metric or decorative marketing asset was added.
- QA identified one readability issue in 10px archive/case metadata. Those labels were raised to 11px without changing the surrounding layout.
