## Who should be named

Good candidates for named profiles are people who:

- hold ongoing company or project accountability;
- can accurately describe their relationship to Venus Bridge Media;
- have approved both languages, image and public role;
- are likely to remain a stable contact or leadership function;
- can evidence the professional experience used in the biography.

Founder / Managing Director, Creative & Production Lead and Operations / Project Coordination may be named only when the real person, relationship and copy are confirmed.

## Who should be role-only

Use a role-only description when:

- the person does not want their name public;
- the role rotates by project;
- the relationship is short-term or confidential;
- a client/artist/project would be exposed by the biography;
- name, title or photo approval is incomplete.

Role-only cards describe responsibilities, not an invented anonymous employee.

## External collaborators

Photographers, videographers, makeup artists, hair stylists, fashion stylists, models, actors, presenters, interview teams, event crew, editors, post-production specialists, drivers and local suppliers should normally be described as the **Extended Production Network**.

- Do not describe a freelancer, agency, venue or supplier as a full-time employee.
- Do not imply every listed role is available or booked for every project.
- Do not publish private contact details, rates, availability or unapproved portfolio media.
- A named collaborator needs their own public approval plus relevant client/project approval.
- Use the approved statement: “Our extended team is assembled according to the needs of each project.” / “项目协作团队会根据每个项目的实际需求进行配置。”

## Image selection

### Best for About Us

- team coordinating a real production;
- production preparation or call briefing;
- camera, lighting or sound setup with people working;
- backstage/event coordination;
- makeup, hair or wardrobe preparation in a professional context;
- authentic London project environment;
- real planning meeting where everyone shown has consented.

### Better kept on case pages

- client-branded set images;
- recognisable artist, model or presenter images;
- venue/event-specific images;
- product/vehicle close-ups;
- final campaign frames;
- technical diagrams or delivery screenshots specific to one project.

### Do not use

- stock-office handshakes;
- generic skyline or globe imagery;
- AI-generated employees or fake group photos;
- unapproved customer images;
- consumer portraits presented as staff;
- luxury-product still life unrelated to actual work.

## Safe no-image fallback

The component displays the approved VB brand mark when an approved named member has no image. Until named profiles are approved, the page shows three core responsibility functions and the project-based production network. This is intentionally more credible than invented biographies or synthetic headshots.
