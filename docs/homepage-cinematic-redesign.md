# Homepage Cinematic Redesign

## Narrative order

1. Positioning: London production for brands entering the UK and Europe.
2. Practical proof: location, bilingual delivery, UK-wide execution, content/talent/events and white-label support.
3. Capabilities: CREATE, CAST, ACTIVATE and LOCALISE.
4. Project governance: disclosed concept models, separated from verified portfolio work.
5. Production process: brief, plan, execution and handoff.
6. Audience routes: brand, agency and specialist teams.
7. Sector context: text rail instead of unapproved logos.
8. Closing action: full project brief or quick enquiry.

## Art direction

The hero combines a portrait campaign frame, supporting detail frame, production code, shot number, timecode, aspect-ratio label and London coordinate. The composition remains usable with fallback media and has an explicit production release gate for authentic replacement assets.

## Commercial clarity

The primary action starts a UK project. The secondary action opens work. The closing section offers a detailed brief and a lower-friction quick enquiry without exposing public commercial ranges.
