# Phase 3.3 Commercial IA Plan

Date: 18 August 2026

## Perception objective

Primary: **International credibility and UK execution partner**.  
Secondary: **premium creative production and project delivery**.  
The site must not imply regulated market-entry advice, official institutional endorsement, event ownership or measured outcomes that the evidence does not support.

## Canonical journey

1. **Home** — concise positioning, named UK/European project proof, three client objectives, delivery logic, selected work, capability hierarchy, reasons to engage, one brief CTA.
2. **Capabilities** — four controlled capabilities ordered by current evidence strength: industry presence; creative production; creators/talent; institutional/research/expert collaboration. Keep the four engagement models but qualify the credibility model until direct evidence exists.
3. **Work** — separate named client projects from creative-capability evidence; expose place, year when known, activity and supported role; include all nine public records.
4. **Case detail** — context, activity, Venus Bridge role, confirmed outputs only, visible evidence and an explicit scope boundary. Omit unknown outcome fields.
5. **About** — explain operating model and publish accountable functions; keep unapproved founder identity private.
6. **Contact** — ask for client intent and project stage, keep friction low, use a specific “Send Project Brief” action. Publish a direct fallback only after a channel is verified.

## Homepage eight-section sequence

1. Hero: credible international presence through real UK projects.
2. Selected project proof: BYD London and London film first; Munich work explicitly labelled European.
3. Client objectives: build credibility, create a UK brand moment, produce reusable international assets.
4. Delivery logic: objective → credible environment/people → controlled execution → usable evidence.
5. Selected work: a compact mix of UK execution, European event execution and creative production.
6. Capabilities: evidence-weighted order and conditional institutional wording.
7. Why Venus Bridge: bilingual judgement, UK delivery control, rights-aware evidence creation.
8. CTA: project brief, timing and intended audience.

## Model decisions

| Engagement model | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International Credibility Project | REFINE / de-emphasise | Commercially important, but current public evidence is adjacent rather than a complete direct case |
| UK Industry Presence Project | KEEP / lead | Strongest named execution evidence; distinguish UK from European delivery precisely |
| International Brand Activation | KEEP / qualify | Talent and editorial media prove capability, not a full creator campaign |
| UK Brand Content Production | STRENGTHEN | Strong UK-location and wider production evidence with low claim risk |

## Routes and content systems

- Keep bilingual canonical routes: Home, Capabilities, Work, nine case pages, About, Contact, Privacy and Terms.
- Keep legacy service and market-entry URLs redirected to canonical Capabilities anchors.
- Restore the omitted European Road & Lifestyle record to the Work index so no public case is sitemap-only.
- Do not expose concept scenarios as completed work.

## Success criteria

- No geography mismatch in homepage proof labels.
- Every Work item has an intelligible proof type and every detail page distinguishes activity, role and output.
- Institutional capability remains visible without names/logos until its evidence gate passes.
- EN/ZH content has semantic parity; 390, 375 and 320 px layouts remain usable.
- Typecheck, lint, content tests and production build pass; remaining production release failures are documented owner/legal/infrastructure gates.
