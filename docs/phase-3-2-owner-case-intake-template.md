## Accepted source formats

The completed facts may arrive as folders, spreadsheets, CSV, JSON, Markdown, PDFs, contracts, event material, image folders or video references. Preserve the original source and record where each public claim came from. Do not require the owner to convert evidence into a proprietary tool.

## Minimum publication handoff

Before Phase 3.2B considers a public case, the intake should identify:

- a real objective and activity;
- the exact Venus Bridge role and boundary;
- concrete outputs;
- relevant capability/project-type mapping;
- an evidence level;
- approved public wording;
- all name, identity, logo, image, video and publication permissions that the proposed page needs;
- one selected primary evidence strength;
- an owner approval decision.

Incomplete records remain internal.
