# Privacy information required

The current bilingual page contains corrected source-faithful pre-release wording only. Before `PRIVACY_NOTICE_COMPLETE=true`, supply approved aligned EN/ZH source for:

- controller identity and contact details;
- purposes and lawful bases;
- categories of personal data;
- recipients/processors and their roles;
- retention periods or objective criteria;
- international transfers and safeguards;
- data-subject rights and request procedure;
- ICO complaint route;
- effective date and version history;
- online-form workflow, Turnstile, rate-limit and delivery processing where applicable.

`content/legal.ts` represents each item as a value plus source-approval state. No missing section is inferred or published in English alone.
