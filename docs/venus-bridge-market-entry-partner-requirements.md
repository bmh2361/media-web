# Minimum Viable Market-Entry Partner Network

No partner is currently configured for public display. Names and logos remain hidden until the checks below are complete.

## Minimum network

1. Company formation/accounting provider — overseas-client experience; clear scope and fees; ACSP status verified if it performs Companies House identity verification or authorised-agent activity.
2. Commercial solicitor — company structure, contracts, shareholder matters, marketing/data questions, event/talent/content rights and other legal review.
3. Trade mark specialist — appropriately qualified trade mark attorney, solicitor with relevant IP expertise or another verified professional provider.
4. Tax/customs specialist — introduced when sales, imports or employment create a real need.
5. Product-compliance specialist — introduced only for the relevant product category.
6. FCA-authorised/permitted specialist — introduced only where investment, regulated products, fundraising roadshows or financial promotions may be involved.

## Required internal record

- Legal name and Companies House record where applicable.
- Written service scope and excluded services.
- Qualifications, regulator/professional body and current public-register evidence.
- Professional indemnity insurance evidence.
- Referral terms, complaints route and conflicts process.
- Data-sharing purpose, lawful route, access and retention terms.
- Contact and secure-document-transfer process.
- Logo permission and approved public wording.
- Last verification date and next review date.

## Publication gate

`partnered` mode requires every configured public partner to satisfy all checks. A missing qualification, expired evidence, absent insurance, unapproved data terms or missing logo permission blocks partnered publication. Companies House states its public ACSP list is not an endorsement and may be incomplete; due diligence remains necessary.
