# Phase 3.2B Evidence Flywheel

Date: 12 August 2026  
Purpose: make evidence capture part of project delivery so every future public claim can be verified, permissioned and commercially useful.

## Flywheel

```text
Project
↓
Evidence captured during delivery
↓
Truth, relationship and rights review
↓
Website credibility
↓
Better-fit client conversation
↓
Stronger project
↓
Stronger evidence
```

The goal is not more website content. The goal is a small body of increasingly strong evidence across Access, Orchestration and Execution.

## Minimum project evidence pack

### Context image

- Show where the work happened.
- Prefer an event, venue, product or environmental view that establishes commercial context.
- Record filename, date, location, photographer/source, rights and the fact the image proves.

### Interaction image

- Show who interacted with whom only where identities and images are publication-safe.
- Record participant roles and the exact relationship.
- A professor, creator, executive or attendee must not be presented as an institutional or brand endorsement.

### Execution image

- Show the work being delivered: event environment, briefing, coordination, production, interview, activation or output.
- Record the narrow Venus Bridge role supported by the image.

### Project description

- Record the client objective, business context, UK activity and desired outcome.
- Keep unknown details blank or `UNKNOWN`.

### Venus Bridge role

- Separate coordination, production and relationship roles.
- Record what Venus Bridge did not do when a wider ownership inference is likely.

### Output record

- List actual delivered items, formats and versions.
- Separate content, event, relationship and brand-asset outputs.
- Record China-facing or stakeholder use only when confirmed.

### Rights record

- Record case publication, client name/logo, institution name/logo, participant name/image, creator identity, venue/event identity, image and video permissions.
- Store exact approved public wording and claim restrictions.
- Record who approved it and when.

### Results record

- Record only verifiable results, their source and publication permission.
- Absence of a metric is acceptable. Do not convert an intended outcome into an achieved result.

## Proof-type capture

| Proof type | Capture priority | Typical public presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual | context, interaction, execution and output imagery | large selected media with concise commercial framing |
| Relationship | objective, engagement format, participant roles, exact relationship, outputs and permissions | editorial facts and metadata; imagery optional |
| Process | objective translation, participant selection logic, agenda, bilingual coordination, logistics and documentation method | explicitly labelled process proof; never presented as a delivered case |

## Capability-specific evidence targets

- **Institutional & Expert Collaboration:** relationship level, institution/participant roles, engagement format, formal agreement status, approved wording and confidentiality boundary.
- **Industry Presence & Events:** event context, booth/launch scope, local coordination, staffing, stakeholder/executive content and final deliverables.
- **Creators, Talent & Cultural Partnerships:** paid/commissioned/organic relationship, casting brief, talent role, usage and disclosure rights.
- **Creative Production & Brand Assets:** objective, production scope, selected outputs, use channels, licensing and measurable results where available.

## Project close-out workflow

1. Project owner completes the Phase 3.2 intake record.
2. Evidence reviewer checks sources and assigns Level A–D.
3. Rights reviewer confirms identities, wording and media permissions.
4. Commercial reviewer maps the frozen capability and project type.
5. Media reviewer selects different hero, card, capability and detail images where the evidence supports it.
6. Case is scored and given one maturity state: Private, Anonymous Public, Named Public or Featured.
7. Owner approves exact public wording and placement.
8. Phase 3.2B/3.2C publication validation must pass before release.

## Confidential engagement rule

Anonymous publication is allowed only when the underlying project and Venus Bridge role are documented, anonymous disclosure is contractually safe, wording cannot identify restricted parties, and no imagery implies their identity. Otherwise the record remains Private and may contribute only to explicitly non-case Process Proof.

## Evidence selection discipline

For a visual-heavy case, score each candidate image against commercial context, subject clarity, brand/event recognition, execution evidence, composition, crop resilience, colour compatibility, desktop suitability, mobile suitability and duplicate value. Aesthetic strength alone is not sufficient.

Do not recolour documentary material in a way that reduces accuracy. Use crop, layout, background, pairing, spacing and scale to fit the site.
