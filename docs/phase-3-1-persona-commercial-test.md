# Phase 3.1 Persona Commercial Test

Date: 12 August 2026  
Method: adversarial 10-second and short-visit review of the Phase 3.1 commercial sequence. The test seeks hesitation and missing proof rather than assuming the positioning succeeds.

## Visitor A — Chinese technology founder seeking international technical credibility

- **10-second interpretation:** Venus Bridge can design a legitimate UK credibility project combining an appropriate professional conversation or visit with executive content and documentation.
- **Trust section:** Four Project Types, specifically International Credibility Project; the claim boundary on Institutional & Expert Collaboration prevents false endorsement signals.
- **Likely project:** International Credibility Project.
- **Missing proof:** No Level A public academic, expert or institutional case.
- **Likely hesitation:** Whether Venus Bridge can currently identify an appropriate participant and coordinate the engagement, not only film it.
- **CTA:** Discuss a UK Collaboration.
- **Adversarial verdict:** Relevant route is clear, but proof is Level C/B. Copy must remain conditional and unnamed until the owner supplies a case.

## Visitor B — Chinese automotive brand preparing for a European exhibition

- **10-second interpretation:** Venus Bridge can support presence in a UK/European industry environment and turn it into launch, exhibition and stakeholder content.
- **Trust section:** Early proof and Selected Work; Changan, BYD, CATL and Leapmotor directly show major automotive event environments.
- **Likely project:** UK Industry Presence Project.
- **Missing proof:** The public cases do not prove complete stand operations, guest management, staffing or event strategy ownership.
- **Likely hesitation:** Exact boundary between local event coordination and event-content production.
- **CTA:** View Selected Work first, then Discuss a UK Collaboration.
- **Adversarial verdict:** Strongest current buyer fit. The site should use event/documentation role labels, never full event ownership unless a new case supports it.

## Visitor C — Chinese premium consumer brand wanting London creators, models and production

- **10-second interpretation:** Venus Bridge can assemble commissioned talent, styling, a London environment and production around one brand activation.
- **Trust section:** International Brand Activation plus fashion/beauty capability evidence.
- **Likely project:** International Brand Activation.
- **Missing proof:** No publishable end-to-end creator campaign or named consumer-brand activation.
- **Likely hesitation:** Whether Venus Bridge provides creator campaign management or primarily visual production and casting coordination.
- **CTA:** Discuss a UK Collaboration.
- **Adversarial verdict:** Relevant route is clear, but the proof is adjacent. “May combine” and “commissioned” must remain; exclusive/top-tier network language is prohibited.

## Visitor D — Chinese company considering UK academic/expert engagement but not market entry

- **10-second interpretation:** Venus Bridge is prepared to design an engagement around a concrete business objective without presenting it as market entry or purchased endorsement.
- **Trust section:** Institutional & Expert Collaboration and International Credibility Project.
- **Likely project:** International Credibility Project.
- **Missing proof:** A permissioned public engagement with an institution or expert.
- **Likely hesitation:** Availability, participant quality, relationship legitimacy and permission boundaries.
- **CTA:** Discuss a UK Collaboration.
- **Adversarial verdict:** The route is now explicit without a new funnel. Conversion depends on owner-supplied Level A proof; the website cannot solve that gap with copy.

## Visitor E — Chinese marketing director wanting premium overseas content for China

- **10-second interpretation:** Venus Bridge can execute UK-located photography, film, interviews or event documentation and deliver reusable commercial assets.
- **Trust section:** UK Brand Content Production and the London automotive film/event cases.
- **Likely project:** UK Brand Content Production.
- **Missing proof:** Approved distribution examples or measurable China-market content outcomes.
- **Likely hesitation:** Deliverable formats, usage licensing, timeline and production scale—questions appropriate for direct scoping.
- **CTA:** View Selected Work, then Discuss a UK Collaboration.
- **Adversarial verdict:** Strong direct-proof fit. Project framing prevents the site reading as a standalone photographer portfolio.

## Cross-persona failure test

All five visitors can identify a relevant project type without adding a homepage section or reintroducing client-stage consulting. Visitors A, C and D still encounter genuine proof gaps; the correct response is the owner evidence pipeline, not stronger claims. Visitors B and E receive direct Level A visual proof and provide the clearest near-term commercial conversion routes.

## Internal commercial perception test

- **£300 resource-broker perception:** Low. No public catalogue, named access list, unit pricing or supplier menu exists.
- **£1,000 photographer perception:** Reduced but not eliminated by the proof library. Project types, strategic ordering and capability metadata frame production as the asset engine behind wider activity.
- **£2,000 generic event-supplier perception:** Low. The site leads with client outcome and cross-capability orchestration rather than event logistics alone.
- **Substantive multi-component B2B project perception:** High enough for serious scoping conversations, strongest for industry presence and brand-content production. Institutional/expert and creator-led programmes need Level A owner evidence to reach the same confidence.

## Architecture decision

No additional route or homepage section is required. The four project types belong inside the existing Capabilities programme section; evidence remains on the existing Work and selected-proof surfaces. This preserves the Phase 3 category and reduces decision cost.
