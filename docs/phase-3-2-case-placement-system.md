# Phase 3.2A Case Placement System

Date: 12 August 2026  
Purpose: decide where verified cases belong without increasing the frozen homepage or publishing an unlimited archive.

## Decision order

1. **Truth gate:** confirm the project is real, the role is precise, and the evidence level is A or carefully approved B.
2. **Rights gate:** confirm case, name, identity, wording and media permissions relevant to the proposed placement.
3. **Story gate:** confirm objective, activity, Venus Bridge role, outputs and commercial relevance.
4. **Score:** use the Phase 3.2 scoring rubric as comparative evidence, not an automatic publishing rule.
5. **Human placement judgement:** select the smallest placement that proves the intended claim.
6. **Owner approval:** approve exact wording, media and placement before publication.

A famous name cannot override weak role evidence or missing rights.

## Placement model

| Placement | Purpose | Eligibility | Capacity / rule | Presentation angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage Early Proof | Immediate credibility | Normally Level A; recognisable or highly credible context; exact substantial role; strong media; rights confirmed | 3 visible now; hard ceiling 4. A stronger case replaces a weaker one. | Project, place/year, narrow role, one decisive visual. |
| Homepage Selected Work | Coherent breadth | Hero or Core evidence; strong buyer relevance; adds a different commercial objective | Approximately 4–6; never a new section. | Commercial objective plus a different visual angle from Early Proof. |
| Capabilities Evidence | Prove one frozen capability | Level A primary case or approved Level B supporting case; exact capability mapping | Aim for 1 primary plus 1–2 supporting cases per capability. | What the case proves about that capability. |
| Commercial Project Type Evidence | Make a project type tangible | Clear mapping to one of the four existing project types; objective and outputs understood | Prefer 1 strongest proof link per project type, with optional supporting case later. | What a similar engagement may look like; no package promise. |
| Work Index | Curated evidence library | Core or Supporting evidence with useful buyer relevance; Visual evidence only if it strengthens a wider story | Selective. Do not publish simply because work exists. No complex filters now. | Context, concise proof label and capability. |
| Dedicated Case Study | Full commercial narrative | Clear objective, UK activity, meaningful role, concrete outputs, strong evidence/media, rights and commercial relevance | Not every Work item qualifies. | Full evidence narrative with one contextual contact step. |
| Supporting / Related Work | Reinforce a stronger case | Narrow or partial proof related to the primary case | Usually 1–3 related items. | A specific supporting capability or visual moment. |
| Archive Only | Preserve internal history | Real but commercially weak, poorly evidenced, rights-restricted or redundant | Unlimited internally; never public by default. | No public presentation. |

## Replacement rules

- New cases replace weaker homepage proof; they never add a ninth homepage section.
- A permissioned institution/expert + company case should replace the adjacent London brand-film proof for International Credibility.
- A verified end-to-end creator/brand activation should replace Teal Editorial as the project-type proof for International Brand Activation and may replace it in Homepage Selected Work.
- A multi-component non-automotive hero case may replace the weakest of the three automotive Early Proof items to improve breadth.
- Existing narrow production cases may remain in Work or related evidence after losing homepage prominence.

## Dedicated-case qualification

All of the following should normally be present:

- concise client objective;
- credible business/event/institution context;
- actual UK or relevant European activity;
- precise Venus Bridge role and boundary;
- concrete deliverables or relationship outputs;
- one or more frozen capability mappings;
- approved case wording and rights;
- one hero and enough selected evidence to support the story;
- commercial relevance understandable to a target CEO or CMO.

If these are incomplete, use supporting evidence or archive until the gaps are resolved.

## Cross-linking rule

The intended journey is:

`Case → relevant capability/project type context → related work → contact`

Do not render a button for every step. A case page normally needs one capability context link, one related/next project and one contact CTA. Project-type context may be a labelled text association instead of another button.

## Logo and relationship rule

No logo wall is generated from the register. A logo requires explicit permission, clear relationship wording, recognition and commercial relevance. Attendance, venue access, photography or third-party participation cannot become “As seen at”, “Partnered with”, “Trusted by” or “Worked with” without evidence for that exact claim.
