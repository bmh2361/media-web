# Case-study Motion System

## Governance

`CaseStatus` is `verified | anonymised | confidential | concept`.

Each record also declares whether it is real, whether it is featured, client-name disclosure, media approval, role disclosure, evidence state and public disclosure text.

## Page chapters

- cinematic media opening;
- disclosure and project metadata;
- context, brief, role and objective;
- scope, deliverables, approvals and visual direction;
- asymmetric contact sheet;
- project credits and role disclosure;
- full-brief action;
- next-project transition.

## Motion rules

- title and major chapters may reveal once;
- media may use masks or editorial curtain reveals;
- long body text remains static;
- reduced motion preserves the complete chapter order;
- concept disclosure remains visible before and after animation;
- placeholder media is never described as evidence.
