# Phase 4 — Commercial Architecture & Premium Visual System

## A. Problems discovered

- The Phase 3 homepage established real work early, but still moved from three broad outcomes into capability presentation before answering the buyer's full decision sequence.
- The hero proposition began with what Venus Bridge does rather than the brand and product the client already owns.
- Industry credibility and local scene credibility were blended together, obscuring the different commercial job each performs.
- Talent and media capability existed in the approved library but did not have a truth-gated homepage narrative.
- The hero stage used a tall cover crop that cut vehicles at tablet widths. The secondary CTA on dark surfaces inherited a light-surface colour treatment.
- English mobile hero copy occupied 574px before refinement.

## B. Architecture changes

The homepage now follows: proposition → early real proof → four China–UK/Europe project directions → six current client objectives → two credibility types → selected evidence → talent/cultural collaboration → media/interview → asset-compounding process → low-friction conversation.

Early proof remains directly after the hero. Directions are image-led editorial rows rather than SaaS cards. Objectives explicitly map Need → Coordinate → Deliver → Remains.

## C. Copy changes

- Hero starts from the client's existing brand and products.
- Empty internationalisation language was replaced with specific UK/European contexts, resources, execution and durable outputs.
- The process is compressed to four client-readable steps.
- The final CTA starts from an existing objective and does not add qualification friction.
- Chinese and English are authored separately; the English mobile H1 was further compressed after runtime measurement.

## D. Media audit findings

- Root audited: `public/media`.
- Assets inspected: 1,050 (542 WebP, 364 AVIF/HEIF, 107 JPEG, 37 SVG).
- Orientation: 672 landscape, 362 portrait, 16 square.
- Exact duplicate files: 20; 444 normalised source/derivative groups.
- 1,015 assets are referenced through source or generated registries.
- 115 contact sheets were generated by folder plus paginated landscape, portrait, square and homepage-candidate views.
- Inventory: `audit/phase-4-media/media-inventory.json`.
- Summary: `audit/phase-4-media/summary.json`.
- Contact sheets: `audit/phase-4-media/contact-sheets/`.

Automated scoring covers commercial relevance, visual quality, premium feel, international context, focal clarity, composition flexibility, crop safety and colour compatibility on a 0–5 scale. Final homepage choices were manually checked in the relevant project, creator, performance and interview sheets; scores did not override evidence meaning or rights status.

## E. Images replaced

- Hero LCP: BYD BD11 London launch, chosen for recognisable product, event and UK evidence without baked-in film titles.
- Hero rotation: Leapmotor IAA exhibition, approved robotics exhibition and approved live interview/panel content.
- Directions: CATL technical presentation, Leapmotor exhibition, approved live-performance editorial and London automotive location production.
- Credibility: CATL stage/audience for industry credibility; Changan launch environment for scene credibility.
- Talent: one performance-led portrait with automotive creator and product-led creator supports.
- Media: approved panel/interview plus a UK-location interview frame.

## F. Crop / focal-point fixes

- Extended the existing `ArtDirectedImage`/presentation-profile system; no duplicate media component was introduced.
- Hero media now fills its stage correctly through the picture element and uses `contain` for protected vehicles, products, people and stage content.
- Mobile remains on one static lead scene; desktop crossfade is low frequency and reduced-motion disables it.
- Removed visible carousel controls. Screen-reader-only controls retain direct scene access and `aria-pressed` state.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile retain registry object positions and purpose-built derivatives.

## G. New credibility architecture

Industry credibility answers why the market should trust the company's professional capability. Market/scene credibility answers why the market should believe the brand is genuinely operating locally. The sections use distinct technical/institutional and spatial/human visual languages within the same restrained system.

## H. Talent section changes

Added project-based talent, ambassador, artist, creator, campaign talent and appearance coordination. The public copy explicitly states that displayed capability does not imply representation, exclusivity or an existing ambassador relationship.

## I. Media section changes

Added UK media, European Chinese-language media, industry media, executive/product interviews, event reporting, press assets and editorial storytelling as capability. No Europe Times logo or partner claim is used because a directly approved relationship record was not found in the public evidence set.

## J. Responsive changes

- English mobile H1 reduced from 574px to 200px at 390px; Chinese renders in five lines at 390px and four at 430px.
- Hero media is a single static lead on mobile and a contained editorial stage on tablet/desktop.
- Dark-surface secondary CTAs now meet the intended contrast.
- Six required viewport sizes were checked in both languages with no horizontal overflow.

## K. Motion changes

The existing motion system remains. Hero media changes every 6.8 seconds only on desktop, pauses on pointer presence and stops under reduced motion. Visible carousel chrome was removed. No new parallax, sticky transformation, 3D or scroll hijacking was added.

## L. Truth-gate decisions

- BYD, Changan, CATL and Leapmotor are described only using approved photography/content roles; no event ownership, market-entry ownership or commercial result is implied.
- Institution and expert capability remains conditional and is not described as endorsement.
- Talent imagery is capability evidence, not a representation roster.
- Media coordination is a capability; no named publication partnership is claimed.
- No statistics, testimonials, awards, institution logos or media logos were invented.

## M. Remaining human inputs

- Approved Europe Times or other named-media evidence, relationship type, published URL/screenshot and wording permission if a named publication should appear.
- Contracted relationship and public-use wording for any named ambassador, artist or creator.
- Commercially complete case inputs: original objective, exact deliverables, approved use channels, measurable/repeat value and client-approved result wording.
- The Phase 3.4 production-release inputs remain outstanding: approved domain/legal identity, live contact destination, distributed rate limiting and owner sign-offs.
